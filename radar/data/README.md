City points are extracted from [GeoNames cities5000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities5000.zip), retrieved October 2, 2026 in America/Los_Angeles. Included states: California, Nevada, Arizona.

GeoNames data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Coordinates and names are retained, with fields reduced to the app's city lookup needs. Data is provided as-is; a city's representative point cannot confirm a seller's distance. The app labels these distances as approximate and requires range confirmation.
